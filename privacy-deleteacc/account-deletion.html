Account Deletion

How to request deletion of your Fores account and the personal data associated with it, for both the Fores website and the Fores mobile application.

Users of the Fores mobile app and fores.kz website can request permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time. This page explains the exact steps to do so.

§ 01

How to Request Deletion

You can request deletion of your Fores account using either of the following methods:

  1. Send a deletion request by email Email info@fores.kz from the address registered on your account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Include the phone number or email used to sign up so we can locate your account.
  2. Request deletion in the app Open the Fores app → go to Profile → Settings → Account, and select "Delete Account". Confirm the request when prompted.
Note: for company accounts (accounts verified via IIN/BIN), deletion also removes the linked company profile and any special pricing or approval status attached to it.
§ 02

What Gets Deleted

Once a deletion request is confirmed, the following data is permanently removed from our active systems:

Data typeStatus
Full name and surnameDeleted
Email address and phone numberDeleted
Account credentials (password hash)Deleted
Delivery addressesDeleted
IIN / company verification dataDeleted
Device tokens / push notification dataDeleted
§ 03

What Is Retained, and Why

Some records are kept for a limited additional period where required by Kazakhstani tax, accounting, or consumer-protection law:

Data typeStatus
Order and invoice recordsRetained — 5 years
Payment transaction recordsRetained — 5 years

This data is retained solely to meet legal and financial reporting obligations, is disconnected from your name/contact profile where possible, and is not used for marketing or any other purpose after account deletion.

§ 04

Processing Time

Deletion requests are processed within 30 days of confirmation. You will receive an email confirming that your account and associated data have been deleted once the process is complete.
